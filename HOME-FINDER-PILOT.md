# Agency-first Home Finder pilot

The offer is one 90-day WNY Brokerage Account installation sold through one
Agency Partner for **$3,000**, with provider/MLS/email/hosting fees billed
separately. After the pilot, an active installation is **$49/month** plus those
external fees. Strelva supports the Agency Partner; the agency supports its
Brokerage Account. There is no direct brokerage sales route before Product Proof.

Pilot Proof exists only when a paying Agency Partner has one WNY Brokerage
Account installed on its real site and a consented test inquiry has a confirmed
delivery event plus a content-free Delivery Receipt.

Product Proof exists only if that first installation renews and a second paid
installation begins within the following 90 days. Until then, configuration is
manual, the architecture stays pilot-sized, and `/ops` remains a separate Labs
experiment rather than a bundled product promise.
